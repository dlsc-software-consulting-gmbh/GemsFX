# Avatar images

The files `src/main/resources/com/dlsc/gemsfx/demo/avatar-1.jpg` ... `avatar-6.jpg` show AI generated portraits, they do not depict
real people. They were taken from the **AGFD-20K** data set (A Generated Face Dataset,
generated with Stable Diffusion):

- Source: https://github.com/Robin-WZQ/AGFD-20K
- License: MIT License, Copyright (c) 2023 Robin-WZQ

The images have been cropped and scaled for use as avatars in the GemsFX demo applications.
